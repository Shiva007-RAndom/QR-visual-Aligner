1. Physical Hardware Architecture & Interconnections

Autonomous visual servoing on a multirotor requires electrical isolation between motor power buses and digital computing rails to eliminate brownouts, EMF noise spikes, and ground loops during high-current flight maneuvers.

Figure 1: Full System Electrical Schematic, Power Distribution & Inter-Process Communication Topology.
POWER RULE 1: NEVER POWER PI 5 FROM PIXHAWK / CUBE: The Cube internal 5V rail is strictly rated for 1A max (for internal IMUs, barometers, and RC receivers). The Pi 5 draws up to 5A under high CPU load. It must be powered exclusively from the dedicated 5V/5A BEC wired to the main battery.

Safety Pilot RC Manual Overrides

The safety pilot retains instantaneous override capability via transmitter switches:

  • Flight Mode Switch (Channel 5 - 3 Position):
    • Pos 1 (High): ALT_HOLD (Full manual throttle & attitude).
    • Pos 2 (Mid): LOITER (Rock-solid GPS position hover).
    • Pos 3 (Low): GUIDED (Autonomous script control).
  • Emergency BRAKE Switch (Channel 7 or 8):
    • Dedicated toggle switch assigned to BRAKE mode. Flipping this switch instantaneously halts the drone in place, immediately overriding companion automation.

4-Rotation Invariant Module Grid Correlation Formula

Candidate QR modules are extracted as a $21 \times 21$ boolean module matrix $M_{\text{cand}}$. The similarity score is computed across all four discrete rotations:

Score = max_{k ∈ {0°, 90°, 180°, 270°}} [ (1 / 441) * ∑_{i=0}^{20} ∑_{j=0}^{20} (M_ref[i,j] == rot90(M_cand, k)[i,j]) ]

When Score >= 0.88 (or decoded payload matches AEROTHON_QR_02), the twin candidate is confirmed, instantly initiating ArduPilot BRAKE mode.

Cable 2 & 3: SIYI RJ45 & Dedicated Pi 5 BEC

  • SIYI Ethernet RJ45: Connect the 8-pin aviation connector from the SIYI gimbal base directly to the Pi 5's onboard RJ45 Gigabit Ethernet port. (Pre-configured static profile siyi-eth @ 192.168.144.50/24).
  • Pi 5 Dedicated BEC: Connect a 5V/5A step-down BEC directly from the main LiPo. Connect its 5.1V output to Pi 5 USB-C or GPIO Pin 2 (+5V) and Pin 6 (GND).
  • Ground Tie: Ensure a 22AWG ground wire bridges the battery ground, Pi 5 ground, Cube ground, and SIYI ground.
